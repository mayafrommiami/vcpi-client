## Why MACCS Keys Help

MACCS keys (166 named structural bits) capture pharmacophoric features — ring types, heteroatom patterns, functional groups — that Morgan FPs encode only implicitly through circular substructure hashing. In THP-1 cells (monocyte/macrophage), many drug mechanisms are driven by specific pharmacophoric patterns (e.g. kinase inhibitor scaffolds, immunomodulator ring systems). MACCS+Morgan gives the Ridge model a richer vocabulary to map structure → transcriptional response.

---

## Evaluation Framework

- **Metric**: weighted MSE (wMSE), lower = better. Genes weighted by variance across compounds.
- **Holdout**: 3 tvc-qnu-012 plates selected by Tanimoto similarity to test compounds.
- **Reference scope**: tvc-qnu-012 active compounds only (qnu-ref) — consistently beats all-dataset reference.
- **Script**: `scripts/eval_fp_comparison.py` for FP head-to-head; `scripts/qnu_plate_holdout_eval.py` for full sweeps.
- **Organizer baseline**: 0.507 (per-gene training mean). Our best: **0.47753** (6.2% improvement).
